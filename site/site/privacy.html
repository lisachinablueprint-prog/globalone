Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Oriental One Group Pte. Ltd. (UEN 202408635N), registered at 60 Paya Lebar Road, #07-54 Paya Lebar Square, Singapore 409051, trading as Oriental One Group (“we”, “us”), collects, uses, protects and keeps your personal data. We follow Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

What we collect

We only collect what we need. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and company.
  • Details you share in an enquiry or consultation booking.
  • If you are a job candidate: your CV, work history, qualifications, referee details and job preferences.
  • Basic, anonymous information about how our website is used.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide our services.
  • To assess job candidates and match them with suitable roles.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for any purpose you have not agreed to.

If you send us your CV

We use your CV only to consider you for suitable roles. We will speak with you before sharing your CV or details with any employer, and we will only share them with your permission.

You can ask us at any time to update your details or remove your CV from our records.

Your consent

By sending us your details, you agree to us using them as described in this policy. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. We will then stop using your personal data, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Who we share it with

We only share personal data:

  • With a prospective employer, if you are a candidate and have given your permission.
  • With trusted service providers who help us run our business (such as email and IT providers), who must keep it secure and confidential.
  • When required by law.

Transfers outside Singapore

We support clients across Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and China, so your personal data may be transferred outside Singapore. When this happens, we make sure it is protected to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

Keeping your data safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, use, loss or disclosure. Access is limited to people who need it to do their work.

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as required by law. CVs are kept for up to [2 years] after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. After that, we securely delete or destroy them.

Accessing and correcting your data

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. We may charge a reasonable fee for access requests, and we will tell you the fee before we proceed.

Data breaches

If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Commission where required by the PDPA.
